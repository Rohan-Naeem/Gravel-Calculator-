About Gravel Calculator

A straightforward tool for estimating gravel volume before you buy.

Purpose of This Website

Gravel Calculator was built to remove the guesswork from planning a gravel project. Rather than estimating by eye or relying on rough rules of thumb, you can enter your project's exact measurements and get an area and volume estimate in seconds, right in your browser.

Who This Calculator Is For

This tool is useful for homeowners planning a driveway or garden path, landscapers quoting a job, contractors double-checking a supplier's numbers, and anyone tackling a DIY patio, walkway, or drainage project. No account or download is required.

How the Calculations Work

The calculator takes the length, width, and depth you enter, converts them into feet if needed, and multiplies them to find volume in cubic feet. That figure is then divided by 27 to express it in cubic yards, and a waste percentage you choose is applied on top to account for settling and material loss. The full breakdown is available on the formula page.

Why Results Are Estimates

Every calculation here is based purely on geometry — the space your project occupies. It does not account for the density of a specific gravel type, moisture content, ground compaction, or irregularities in your site. Those factors all influence how much material you'll actually use, which is why we recommend treating these numbers as a solid starting point and confirming final quantities with your supplier.
