Estimates Only

All results produced by the gravel calculator on this website are estimates intended for general planning purposes. They are calculated purely from the length, width, depth, and waste percentage you provide, using standard geometric formulas.

Material Density Varies

Different gravel and aggregate types have different densities. This calculator estimates volume, not weight, so it does not account for how much a specific material weighs per cubic yard.

Moisture Affects Weight

Gravel that is wet weighs more than the same gravel when dry. Moisture content can vary by delivery, season, and storage conditions, which affects any weight-based estimate you calculate separately from this tool.

Compaction Affects Volume

Loose gravel occupies more space than gravel that has been compacted. As material settles or is compacted during installation, the effective depth or footprint on site can differ from the calculator's geometric assumptions.

Site Conditions Vary

Uneven ground, slopes, existing subgrade material, and irregular boundaries all affect how much gravel a real project requires compared to the simple rectangular calculation used here.

Verify Before You Buy

Before placing a large order, we recommend verifying your estimated quantity with a gravel supplier, contractor, or other qualified professional who can account for your specific material choice and site conditions. Gravel Calculator and its results should not be treated as professional engineering, construction, or purchasing advice.
