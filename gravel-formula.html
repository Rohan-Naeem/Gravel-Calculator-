Gravel Calculator Formula

The math behind area, volume, cubic yards, and waste adjustment.

Area Formula

Area is the flat surface you plan to cover, calculated from length and width in the same unit.

Area = Length × Width Result is in square feet when length and width are in feet.

Volume Formula

Volume adds depth to the area calculation, giving the total space the gravel needs to fill.

Volume = Length × Width × Depth Result is in cubic feet when all three inputs are in feet.

Cubic Feet to Cubic Yards

Gravel is commonly sold by the cubic yard. Since one cubic yard equals 27 cubic feet, divide the cubic feet total by 27.

Cubic Yards = Cubic Feet ÷ 27

Depth and Unit Conversion

If your measurements are not already in feet, convert them first. The calculator applies the following conversions internally:

1 meter = 3.280839895 feet 1 inch = 1 ÷ 12 foot   |   1 centimeter = 1 ÷ 30.48 foot

Waste / Extra Material Adjustment

Because gravel settles, compacts, and is lost during spreading, it's common to add an extra percentage on top of the base volume before ordering.

Adjusted Volume = Volume × (1 + Waste Percentage ÷ 100)

Worked Example

Driveway, 20 ft × 10 ft, 3 in deep, 10% extra

  • Depth in feet: 3 ÷ 12 = 0.25 ft
  • Area: 20 × 10 = 200 sq ft
  • Volume: 200 × 0.25 = 50 cubic feet
  • Cubic yards: 50 ÷ 27 = 1.85 cubic yards
  • Adjusted volume (10% extra): 50 × 1.10 = 55 cubic feet
  • Adjusted cubic yards: 55 ÷ 27 = 2.04 cubic yards
