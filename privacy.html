Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Gravel Calculator handles information when you use this website. We aim to keep the calculator simple and to be clear about what does and does not happen with the numbers you enter.

Calculator Inputs

The length, width, depth, unit, and waste percentage values you enter into the calculator are processed directly in your web browser using JavaScript. These values are used only to perform the on-page calculation and are not sent to a server as part of the calculator's normal operation, unless additional services described below are added in the future.

Information We Do Not Collect

At this time, this website does not use analytics, advertising, cookies, contact forms, or third-party tracking services. We do not currently collect names, email addresses, or other personal information through this site.

Future Changes

If analytics, advertising, contact forms, cookies, or other third-party services are added to this website in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe what data is collected, how it is used, and what choices are available to visitors. Please check this page periodically for updates.

Third-Party Links

This site does not currently link to third-party checkout, payment, or account services. If external links are added in the future, we recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any external site you visit.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the site owner through the contact details published on this domain.
